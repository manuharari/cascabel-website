Sports Intelligence

We don't replace your methodology.

Every club is different. Every coaching staff thinks about the game differently. Cascabel Sports Intelligence doesn't impose a model — it augments the one you already have with artificial intelligence, advanced analytics and decision-support tools built around how your club actually operates. It's a division of cascabel.io, not a different company or a different brand: the same way of working, applied to professional football.

How we work

From diagnosis to implementation in weeks

1

Diagnosis

A diagnosis call with your coaching staff or sporting director to identify which module solves the most urgent problem. No cost, no commitment.

2

Deep Dive

A deep review of your data, workflow and current methodology — video, GPS, scouting, club ERP — to define what to build, why, and in what order.

3

Integration & validation

Integration with your existing systems. The module runs in parallel with your current operation. Your staff validates until they trust it.

4

Go-live

First autonomous cycle within your competitive calendar. We scale to more modules when the club is ready.
